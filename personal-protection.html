The six protection areas

What personal digital protection actually covers

Home cybersecurity is not one product — it's six connected areas. A weakness in any one of them can expose the rest. Here's what we look at, and why each one matters.

Devices
Phones, tablets and computers hold your photos, messages, banking and work. We check updates, screen locks, encryption and built-in protections — and configure what's missing, with you watching.
Home Wi-Fi & smart technology
Your router, smart TVs, cameras, doorbells and speakers all connect to the same network. We review router settings, default passwords and what's connected — and set up a guest network where it helps.
Passwords & online accounts
One weak or reused password can unlock everything. We strengthen your key accounts — email, banking, Apple ID, Google — with unique passwords, multi-factor sign-in and working recovery options.
Personal privacy
Digital privacy means controlling what strangers, apps and data brokers can learn about your household. We review app permissions, social sharing and public exposure — then help you reduce it.
Identity & fraud exposure
Impersonation messages, fake bank calls and "urgent" requests are now everyday events. We build your household's scam readiness: verification habits, warning signs, and a clear plan if something goes wrong.
Family digital safety
Protection that spans generations — practical safeguards for kids' devices, guidance for teens, and patient, respectful help for aging parents, arranged with their consent.
