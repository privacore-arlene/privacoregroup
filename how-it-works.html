How it works

A calm process, from first hello to written plan.

No pressure, no jargon, no surprises. Here is exactly what happens when you work with PrivaCore — and the safeguards that keep you in control throughout.

The four steps

Understand. Assess. Strengthen. Support.

01
Understand
A confidential discussion about the household and concerns.
02
Assess
Review agreed devices, accounts, network settings and digital safety habits.
03
Strengthen
Prioritize and implement appropriate safeguards within the purchased service scope.
04
Support
Provide a written plan, practical education and optional scheduled follow-up.
The full journey

What actually happens, step by step

  1. You reach out. A short form tells us your household situation, location and what prompted you to look into digital safety. No technical detail needed.
  2. We respond within one business day. A real person replies with a few clarifying questions and proposed times for a first conversation.
  3. A confidential first conversation. Free and without obligation — we listen, explain options honestly, and tell you if we're not the right fit.
  4. You receive a written scope and quote. What we'll review, what it costs, what it doesn't include — confirmed in writing before anything is booked.
  5. A limited, secure intake. Just enough information to prepare: household members, device types, general concerns. Never passwords, banking details or recovery codes.
  6. Consent and authorization. You approve exactly what we may look at and what we may change — before we touch anything.
  7. Your scheduled checkup. In person across Metro Vancouver, or remotely by secure video. You stay present and in control; you type your own credentials.
  8. Findings and action plan. A plain-language written report: what we verified, what we fixed together, and what's left — in priority order.
  9. Optional follow-up. Scheduled check-ins if you'd like continued support. Clear written terms, month to month, no lock-in — and never a surprise.
Your safeguards

Consent is built into every step

  • Written scope first. Nothing begins until you've seen and approved exactly what's included.
  • You hold the credentials. We never ask for passwords, banking details, recovery codes or identity documents. When a sign-in is needed, you type it yourself.
  • Changes need your yes. Every adjustment is explained before it's made, and you can decline any of them.
  • Minimal data, held carefully. We collect only what the service requires, handle it under our privacy policy, and never place your security findings in public or unsecured locations.
  • Easy to pause or stop. Confirmed appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled without charge up to 24 hours before they start.
