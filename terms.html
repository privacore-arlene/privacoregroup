Terms of Service

Clear terms, in plain language.

Last updated: October 2026 · C-Success Corp., operating as PrivaCore Group ("PrivaCore", "we", "us")

Our services

PrivaCore provides personal digital safety assessments, implementation guidance and education for households — including the Digital Safety Checkup described on this website. Services are delivered by scheduled appointment, in person within Metro Vancouver or remotely where we confirm we can genuinely support your situation.

Quotes and pricing

  • Published prices are introductory launch pricing in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes.
  • Every engagement is confirmed with a written scope and final quote before work begins. The written quote governs.
  • Additional devices, extended time, travel beyond central Metro Vancouver, and specialist services are quoted separately and proceed only with your written approval.
  • Third-party software, subscriptions and hardware are your responsibility and are billed by their providers, not by us. We do not take commissions on recommendations.

Consent and authorization

  • We review and change only what you authorize in writing, and every change is explained before it is made.
  • We never request passwords, banking details, recovery codes or identity documents. You enter your own credentials at all times.
  • Where a service is arranged for another adult (such as a parent), that person's own consent is required.

Scheduling and cancellation

Confirmed appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled without charge up to 24 hours before they start. If we must reschedule, we'll offer the nearest available alternatives.

What our services are not

  • Not 24/7 or real-time monitoring, and not a guarantee that incidents will never occur
  • Not emergency incident response — in an urgent fraud situation, contact your bank and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501) first
  • Not insurance, legal advice, or a compliance certification
  • Not intrusive testing — we perform no penetration testing or vulnerability exploitation on your home systems

Reasonable limits

We provide services with professional care and skill. Digital safety involves factors outside anyone's control — new threats, provider changes, and everyday human moments — so we cannot warrant that a household will never experience an incident. To the extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the fees paid for the service in question. These terms are governed by the laws of British Columbia and Canada.
