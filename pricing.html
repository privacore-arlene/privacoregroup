How quotes work

What can adjust the starting price

  • Household size and device count — the base price covers one household and up to four devices; larger setups are quoted accordingly.
  • Location and travel — in-person appointments within central Metro Vancouver are included; travel beyond that may add a clearly stated charge.
  • Scope you approve — additional devices, extra time or specialist help are quoted separately and only proceed with your written approval.
  • Taxes — prices are in Canadian dollars, plus applicable taxes.

What we don't do: required subscriptions, automatic renewals, commissions on software we recommend, or add-ons you didn't approve. Optional ongoing support is discussed only after your checkup — with terms in writing, month to month, and no monitoring claims.

Service boundaries

  • PrivaCore provides assessment, implementation guidance and education — not 24/7 monitoring, emergency incident response, or insurance products.
  • Confirmed appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled without charge up to 24 hours before they start.
  • We never ask for passwords, banking details, recovery codes or identity documents, and nothing changes without your authorization.
Pricing questions

Fair questions, straight answers

Why does it say "from" $649?
Because households differ. Four devices in a one-bedroom condo is a different job from three floors, ten devices and a home office. The starting price fits a typical household; anything different is quoted in writing first — you never discover the price after the fact.
Are there any ongoing fees?
None required. The checkup is complete on its own. If you'd like scheduled follow-up support afterwards, we'll offer it separately with clear written terms — month to month, no lock-in, and no automatic renewals.
Do you charge for the first conversation?
No. The initial conversation — understanding your household and whether we're the right fit — is free and carries no obligation.
What if I need help beyond the checkup's scope?
We'll tell you plainly, explain options, and quote any additional work separately. Where a specialist is genuinely needed, we work with independent professionals under confirmed arrangements — with your approval first.
