Privacy Policy

Your privacy is the product we protect. It starts with ours.

Last updated: October 2026 · C-Success Corp., operating as PrivaCore Group ("PrivaCore", "we", "us")

What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to respond to you and deliver services:

  • Inquiry details — name, email, optional phone number, general location or postal area, preferred appointment format, and your message, submitted through our contact form or by email/phone.
  • Service details — with your written consent, information needed to perform a Digital Safety Checkup: household composition relevant to the work, device types, and findings recorded in your report.

We never ask for or store passwords, banking credentials, recovery codes or identity documents. Please don't send them to us.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule services
  • To prepare, deliver and document the services you purchase
  • To meet legal, accounting and insurance obligations

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with marketers. We do not send newsletters or promotional email without your explicit request.

How this website works

This website is hosted on Netlify. Our contact form is processed by Netlify Forms, which stores submissions securely and forwards them to us. This site does not use advertising trackers, and we do not run behavioural analytics. Standard web-server logs (such as IP addresses) are processed by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

Your service findings

Your Digital Safety Checkup report is confidential. It is delivered directly to you, stored with restricted access, and never published, shared or used as a case study without your explicit written permission.

Retention and your rights

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and legal requirements, then securely delete it. Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA and applicable provincial law), you may request access to your personal information, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete it, subject to legal limits.
