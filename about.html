About PrivaCore Group

Experienced Leadership. Personal Attention.

PrivaCore Group is a founder-led Canadian professional practice bringing senior-level privacy and cybersecurity experience to the place it matters most — your home.

The practice

Serious expertise, brought home.

For more than fifteen years, the leadership behind PrivaCore worked where digital protection is unforgiving — privacy and cybersecurity governance, technology risk assessment, financial services and other regulated industries, data protection, and the operational change and client education that make safeguards stick.

PrivaCore was founded on a simple observation: households face the same categories of digital risk as organizations — but are expected to manage them alone, with products instead of guidance. We built the practice we wished existed for our own families: senior-led, personal, practical, and priced for ordinary Canadian households.

"Senior-led digital protection. Personal service. Practical results."
How we work

A professional practice, not a storefront.

Authority comes from method, not marketing. Every PrivaCore engagement is built on:

Clear service methodology
A documented, repeatable process — understand, assess, strengthen, support — so every household gets the same thorough standard.
Professional reporting
Every checkup concludes with a written findings summary and prioritized action plan — plain language, no jargon.
Secure intake and consent
Minimal data collection, written scope, and explicit authorization before anything is reviewed or changed.
Defined service boundaries
We state plainly what we do and don't provide — no 24/7 monitoring, no emergency response, no insurance products.
Privacy-first practice
Your information is handled under Canadian privacy law and our published privacy policy. Your findings are never shared or published.
Independent specialists when needed
Where work exceeds our scope, we can involve independent professionals under confirmed arrangements — always disclosed, always with your approval.
