Experienced risk leadership. Practical business delivery.

Arlene Chamulak founded PrivaCore Group to make the risk practices used by large organizations practical and accessible for smaller businesses.

Arlene Chamulak, founder

Arlene Chamulak has spent 15+ years leading privacy, cybersecurity governance and regulatory remediation programs inside regulated Canadian organizations including RBC, BNP Paribas, WestJet and BC Lotteries.

She founded PrivaCore Group because she saw the same practical gaps in small businesses that large institutions already fix — and wanted to close them without enterprise complexity or fear tactics.
