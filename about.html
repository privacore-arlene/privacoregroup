About PrivaCore Group

Fraud prevention and business protection for Canadian businesses.

PrivaCore Group helps Canadian businesses strengthen the procedures and controls that protect payments, information and day-to-day operations.

What we do

Practical protection, sized to your business.

Services focus on fraud preparedness, supplier and banking-change verification, employee awareness, privacy and governance. Construction, renovation and trade businesses are a particular area of focus, alongside other small and medium-sized businesses.

1
Assess
Understand how payments, information and systems actually move through your business.
2
Prioritize
Turn findings into a clear, plain-language set of priorities — what to fix first, and why.
3
Protect
Put practical controls in place, with specialist technical support coordinated where required.
How we work

Independent assessment. Practical recommendations. Specialist execution.

Each engagement starts with an agreed scope and produces practical findings, priorities and next steps. Where specialist technical expertise is needed, the delivery arrangements and responsibilities are agreed with the client.

Backed by a specialist network. PrivaCore maintains an active network of specialists across cybersecurity, privacy, and fraud review, and draws on it whenever a client's situation calls for it — so every engagement reflects current best practice across the industry.
