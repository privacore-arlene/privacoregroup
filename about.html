Arlene Chamulak, founder

Arlene Chamulak has spent nearly three decades in IT. She started on a help desk and worked her way up to leading privacy, cybersecurity governance, and regulatory remediation programs inside regulated Canadian organizations across banking, oil & gas, insurance, telecommunications, travel, gaming, and retail — over the last 15+ years.

She founded PrivaCore Group because she kept seeing the same gap: small businesses facing the same fraud and security risks as the institutions she'd spent her career protecting, without any of the budget, staff, or in-house expertise to defend themselves. PrivaCore brings that same rigor down to a small-business scale — real plans, plain language, no enterprise complexity and no fear tactics.

Backed by a specialist network. Arlene also maintains an active network of specialists across cybersecurity, privacy law, and fraud investigation, and draws on it whenever a client's situation calls for it — so every PrivaCore engagement reflects current best practice across the industry, not just one person's read on it.
