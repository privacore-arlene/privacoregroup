About PrivaCore Group

Business risk, fraud prevention and digital trust — in plain language.

PrivaCore Group helps Canadian businesses reduce their exposure to fraud, cyber risk, payment diversion, privacy failures and emerging digital threats.

What we do

The gap between IT support and business risk.

We focus on the gap between traditional IT support and business risk. That means looking beyond technology to the people, processes, payment controls, vendors and business decisions that often determine whether a cyber or fraud incident becomes a financial loss.

PrivaCore works alongside existing IT providers and coordinates specialist cybersecurity services where required. Clients receive practical recommendations, clear priorities and access to appropriate technology and security expertise without needing to build an internal security team.

How we work

Independent assessment. Practical recommendations. Specialist execution.

We identify and prioritize risk first.

Where remediation, implementation or managed protection is required, PrivaCore can coordinate appropriate specialist providers and technology partners.

This gives businesses a clear point of accountability while allowing specialist technical work to be performed by providers with the appropriate capabilities.

Backed by a specialist network. PrivaCore maintains an active network of specialists across cybersecurity, privacy, and fraud review, and draws on it whenever a client's situation calls for it — so every engagement reflects current best practice across the industry.
