Common questions

About the checkup

How should I prepare for the appointment?
Simply have the devices you'd like reviewed charged and available, know roughly where your router is, and think about any concerns you've had — a strange message, a forgotten password, a parent's phone habits. We handle the rest.
Can you do this remotely?
Yes — by secure video call, anywhere in Canada where we can genuinely support your situation. Router work is easier in person, so we'll tell you honestly at intake whether remote is a good fit for your home.
What if my household has more than four devices?
No problem — larger households simply adjust the quote. Tell us how many devices and people are involved, and you'll see the final price in writing before you commit.
Is this a one-time thing?
The checkup is a complete, self-contained service. Many households find an annual refresh useful as devices, apps and scams evolve — but nothing auto-renews, and there's no obligation.
Do you offer ongoing support?
Optional scheduled follow-up support is available after your checkup — we'll discuss it only once your report is in hand, with clear terms in writing. We don't offer 24/7 monitoring or emergency response, and we won't claim otherwise.
