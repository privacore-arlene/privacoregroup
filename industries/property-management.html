Industries — Property Management

Protect the information, payments, and vendor relationships that keep properties running.

Property-management work moves quickly between tenants, owners, boards, maintenance vendors, leases, invoices, rent, and service requests. A false payment change, compromised mailbox, lost document, or poorly controlled vendor account can disrupt more than one property at once.

PrivaCore helps property managers and strata-management businesses create practical controls around payment verification, property-management systems, shared records, vendor access, and incident response—without slowing the people who need to keep buildings operating.

Familiar moments in property management

A document looks routine — until it isn't

A maintenance invoice, an owner or board document, a lease attachment, or a contractor form arrives at a busy moment from an address that looks close enough to real. Payment-change impersonation and false-invoice fraud are documented Canadian business-fraud patterns that apply directly to the volume of vendor and owner correspondence a property-management business handles.

Can your team verify the sender and use the right system before opening or forwarding it — rather than acting on the message as it arrived?

Common questions

Property management FAQs

Are property managers uniquely targeted by attackers?
We don't claim that. The case for this pathway is workflow-based: property-management businesses handle other people's money and information, with frequent payment-change requests and many outside vendors — which makes clear verification and access practices worth reviewing.
We use a property-management platform — isn't security the vendor's job?
Partly. The vendor secures their platform; your accounts, staff access, document-sharing habits, and payment practices remain your responsibility. The review covers your side of that line.
Who from our team should be involved?
Usually whoever approves payments, whoever manages vendor and board access, and whoever handles owner and tenant records. We keep the time commitment small and focused.
