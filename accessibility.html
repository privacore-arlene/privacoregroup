Accessibility

Built to be used by everyone.

Digital safety should never depend on how young your eyes are or how steady your hands feel.

Our commitment

We design and test this website toward WCAG 2.2 Level AA, with particular attention to older adults — the people who most often ask us for help. In practice, that means:

  • Readable body text (17px or larger) with generous line spacing
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 against its background
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
  • Touch targets of at least 48px for buttons and links
  • Plain-language labels — no jargon on buttons or forms
  • Respect for reduced-motion settings
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images

If something doesn't work for you

Tell us and we'll fix it — and we'll gladly deliver any information on this site another way: by phone, in larger print, or in person. Accessibility feedback: info@privacoregroup.com · 604-341-1651.
