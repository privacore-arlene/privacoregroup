Readiness & Governance Packages

Scoped packages for common starting points.

Advisory and readiness work, tailored to your organization. Packages help you prepare for audits, assessments and regulatory expectations; they do not guarantee certification, an audit opinion or compliance.

Privacy

Privacy Program Readiness

A review of privacy governance, policies, personal-information inventory, privacy impact assessment processes, retention, third-party privacy risk and incident readiness.

You receive: a prioritized gap summary with owners and practical next steps.

PIPEDA, Québec Law 25, GDPR or other laws are referenced only where they apply to your scope. Not legal advice.

Assurance readiness

SOC 2 Readiness & Controls Remediation

Preparation for a SOC 2 engagement: scope and control mapping, evidence expectations, gap tracking, control-owner coordination, remediation planning and adoption support.

Where your services could affect customers’ financial reporting, SOC 1 readiness is available for those controls.

PrivaCore does not issue SOC reports. An independent CPA firm performs the attestation and issues the report.

Assurance readiness

ISO/IEC 27001 Readiness

Information security management system (ISMS) gap assessment, control and evidence planning, remediation coordination and implementation support.

PrivaCore does not issue ISO certification. An external certification body makes the certification decision.

Cybersecurity

NIST CSF 2.0 Cybersecurity Profile

Define your current and target profiles, identify priority gaps, and build an improvement roadmap with accountable owners. Where identity and authentication are in scope, controls are reviewed against NIST SP 800-63B.

NIST CSF 2.0 is a voluntary framework and NIST SP 800-63B is guidance; neither is a certification.

Responsible AI

Responsible AI Governance Foundation

Inventory AI use cases, clarify accountability, identify privacy and governance risks, develop proportionate policies and review workflows, and support responsible use through stakeholder communication and training—aligned to the NIST AI RMF and, where it applies to you, the EU AI Act.

Not an independent AI audit, legal opinion or technical model validation. The NIST AI RMF is voluntary; EU AI Act obligations should be confirmed with legal counsel.

Adoption

Change & Adoption Support

Stakeholder readiness, communications, role-based training, workflow changes and adoption follow-through, built into privacy, GRC, cybersecurity and AI-governance work. It is how controls become everyday practice—not project administration.

Packages are tailored to the organization’s scope and delivery needs. Where specialized implementation, independent testing, audit, legal advice, or certification is required, roles are agreed with appropriately qualified external providers.
