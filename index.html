Your digital life is connected

Household safety is about more than antivirus software.

Your phones, computers, home Wi-Fi, accounts, privacy settings and family habits all connect — and a weakness in one can expose the rest. Personal digital safety means looking at the whole picture, calmly and practically.

What we help protect

Common questions

Everything households usually ask us.

Do I need to be technical to work with PrivaCore?
Not at all. Our service is built for people who don't want to manage cybersecurity themselves. We explain everything in plain language, do the hands-on work with you, and leave you with a written plan you'll actually understand.
Will you ask for my passwords or banking details?
Never. We don't ask for passwords, banking credentials, recovery codes or identity documents — at intake or during a checkup. When a password needs changing, you type it yourself. That's a permanent rule, not a preference.
Is this just antivirus software installation?
No. Software is only one small layer. A Digital Safety Checkup reviews your home Wi-Fi, devices, key accounts, privacy settings, backup habits and scam readiness — the whole picture of your household's digital safety.
Can you help protect my aging parents?
Yes — this is one of the most common reasons families contact us. Adult children can arrange and attend a checkup for a parent, with the parent's consent. We work patiently, respect their independence, and put practical anti-scam safeguards in place.
Do you offer 24/7 monitoring or emergency response?
No, and we won't pretend to. PrivaCore provides scheduled, expert-led assessments and guidance — not round-the-clock monitoring, emergency incident response or insurance products. If you ever face an urgent fraud situation, we'll point you to your bank and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501) first.
Where do you provide services?
In-person appointments are available across Metro Vancouver. Remote checkups by secure video call are available across Canada where we can genuinely support your situation — we'll tell you honestly at intake if remote is a good fit.
What does the Digital Safety Checkup cost?
Three fixed tiers, as introductory launch pricing: Solo $429 (one person, two devices), Family $599 (a typical household, up to four devices), and Premium $849 (up to eight devices, plus a follow-up call). Anything beyond a tier's scope is quoted in writing first — no obligation, no surprises.
Do you sell or recommend specific products?
We recommend tools where they're genuinely useful — like a password manager — but we don't sell software, take commissions, or earn referral fees. Our advice stays independent.
