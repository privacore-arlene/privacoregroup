Fair questions

Gifting, answered honestly

Do they have to agree to it?
Yes — always. Consent is a firm rule at PrivaCore: we never review or change anything for an adult who hasn't agreed themselves. If your loved one declines after our introduction, we'll tell you kindly and refund the gift before any work begins.
Will I see their report or findings?
No — the written report belongs to them. We'll confirm the visit happened and that they have their plan, but the details stay private. Many families then choose to discuss it together, on the recipient's terms.
What if they're not technical at all?
That's exactly who this is for. Everything is explained in plain language, at their pace, without judgement. They type their own passwords; we never ask for them.
They don't live in Vancouver — can I still gift it?
In most cases, yes. Remote checkups by secure video call are available across Canada where we can genuinely support their situation — we'll confirm that honestly at intake.
