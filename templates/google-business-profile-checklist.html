Google Business Profile — Setup Checklist

Internal guide · Goal: a verified Metro Vancouver local listing + the first 10 genuine customer reviews. This is the highest-leverage acquisition step before any paid marketing.

1. Create and claim the profile

Go to business.google.com and sign in with a Google account you control long-term (ideally one tied to @privacoregroup.com email).
Business name exactly: PrivaCore Group — no keyword stuffing in the name field (Google suspends for that).
Primary category: Computer security service (closest available to household cybersecurity). Secondary: Computer consultant, Security service.
Business type: Service-area business — hide the street address; set service area to Metro Vancouver cities (Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Surrey, Delta, Langley).

2. Verify

Complete Google's verification (video verification is now most common — record a short video showing your workspace, branding, and proof of location).
Use a consistent phone number everywhere: 604-341-1651 — must match the website footer exactly.

3. Complete the profile 100%

Description (750 chars): lead with "Personal digital protection for Canadian households" and naturally include digital safety checkup Vancouver, home cybersecurity, digital privacy, personal digital safety.
Add services with prices: Digital Safety Checkup — Solo $429 / Family $599 / Premium $849 CAD; Gift a Checkup; Annual Refresh.
Website link: https://privacoregroup.com/; appointment link: https://privacoregroup.com/contact.
Photos: upload 5+ — logo, hero-style home imagery, a photo of you working (real, not stock), Vancouver context shot.
Hours: set real availability; note "by appointment" if applicable.

4. The first 10 genuine reviews

In the GBP dashboard, copy your review link (short Google URL).
After each completed checkup, send a personal follow-up message thanking the client and including the review link — ask honestly, never incentivize (Google policy).
Reply to every review within a few days — short, warm, professional.
Target: 10 reviews before spending anything on ads. Reviews + a verified listing outrank a bigger website at this stage.

5. Keep NAP consistent

Name, Address/Service-area, Phone must be identical everywhere: website footer, GBP, The Fraud Doctor site (if referenced), any directory listings. Inconsistency suppresses local ranking.

6. After verification

Submit https://privacoregroup.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and request indexing of the main pages.
Post a first GBP "update" announcing the Digital Safety Checkup launch pricing.
Add 2-3 Q&A entries yourself (e.g. "Do you offer remote appointments?" — answer honestly).
